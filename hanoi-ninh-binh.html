Phố Cổ The Old Quarter & landmarks · days 2–3
  • Hoàn Kiếm Lake at sunrise free

    Locals doing tai chi around the water. Cross the red bridge to Ngọc Sơn Temple on the island ₫50,000 · ₹180

  • The 36 streets free

    Each street was named for the single trade sold there — silk on one, tin on the next. Best walked before 10am, before heat and traffic peak. Motorbikes outnumber pedestrians roughly a hundred to one.

  • Temple of Literature · Văn Miếu ₫70,000 · ₹250

    Vietnam's first university, founded 1070. A night light-show version also runs if we want an evening slot instead.

  • Hỏa Lò Prison — the “Hanoi Hilton” ₫50,000 · ₹180

    French-colonial prison, later held American POWs. Heavy but worth real time; take the audio guide.

  • Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum + One Pillar Pagoda free

    Mornings only, strict dress code, closed Mon & Fri. It's away from the Old Quarter — this is the one Grab ride (~₹100–150).

  • Water puppet show · Múa rối nước ₫100–200,000 · ₹360–720

    Thăng Long theatre by the lake. Book a day ahead — it sells out.

  • Train Street café cost of a coffee

    A train squeezes past the café tables twice a day. Only sit where officially allowed — sections get closed for safety, check status on the day.

  • St. Joseph's Cathedral, Đồng Xuân Market, weekend night market free

    Night market runs Fri–Sun evenings around Hàng Đào street.

Ăn Eat — egg coffee is non-negotiable
  • Cà Phê Giảng

    Egg coffee at the café of the family that invented it in 1946. The trip site calls it non-negotiable.

  • Bún Chả Hương Liên

    The Obama/Bourdain spot — Hanoi's answer to the one in the Ho Chi Minh doc. Grilled pork, noodles, herbs.

  • Phở stalls around Bát Đàn street

    Go early morning. Also on the list: bánh mì, bánh cuốn, coconut coffee.

  • 🌱 Vegetarian

    A whole strip of “chay” (veg-only) eateries along Trần Nhân Tông street. Ask for phở chay (mushroom/tofu broth) and bánh mì chay; many Old Quarter spots will make bánh cuốn veg-friendly on request.

Ngủ Stay — Old Quarter, 3 nights

Budget

₹1,200–1,900 / night

Hostels and mini-hotels inside the Old Quarter. This is our tier if we're saving — split a twin to halve it.

Mid-range

₹3,000–5,500 / night

Hanoi La Siesta, Silk Path — the ones the route site names.

Upscale

₹9,000+ / night

Sofitel Legend Metropole, for the record.

Location rule: stay inside the Old Quarter or by Hoàn Kiếm Lake. Everything above except the Mausoleum is walking distance, so 3 nights here means almost zero transport cost.

Tràng An Ninh Bình day trip · day 4 · “Ha Long Bay on land”

Karst towers rising out of flooded rice paddies, ~95 km south — about 2 hours each way by door-to-door limousine van. Done as one long day trip, which saves a hotel night for Sapa. Group day tours from Hanoi run ~₹1,700–2,600 including van, lunch, entries and a bicycle; DIY is the van ₹550–850 return plus entries below.

  1. Morning Tràng An boat ride ₫250,000 · ₹900, boat incl.

    2–3 hours rowed through cave tunnels carved straight through the rock — the boatwomen often row with their feet. Longer and less crowded than Tam Cốc. (Tam Cốc is the shorter, busier alternative, ₫120–250,000 + a ₫50–100,000 tip for the rower.)

  2. Midday Hoa Lư ancient capital, or cycling the paddies

    Vietnam's 10th-century capital, usually bundled into tours. Bích Động Pagoda is free if we pass it.

  3. Lunch Riverside restaurants near Tam Cốc

    Local specialty is dê núi (mountain goat). Every menu also has a rau (vegetable) and đậu phụ (tofu) section — veg is easy here.

  4. Sunset Hang Múa viewpoint ₫100,000 · ₹360

    ~500 steps up for the classic aerial shot of the river bending through the peaks. This is the photo.

Skip: Bãi Đính Pagoda unless people specifically want a very large temple complex — it eats half the day.
